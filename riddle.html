THE MYSTERY RIDDLE

You are standing outside a closed room. Outside the room, there are 3 switches. Inside the room, there is 1 light bulb.

Only one switch controls the bulb. You may turn the switches on or off as many times as you want, but you may enter the room only once.

How can you determine which switch controls the light bulb?

YOUR SOLUTION

HINT:
Think beyond whether the bulb is on or off. What else can a light bulb tell you after it has been on for a while?
THE SOLUTION

1. Turn switch 1 on and wait several minutes.
2. Turn switch 1 off, then turn switch 2 on.
3. Enter the room once.

• Bulb is ON: Switch 2 controls it.
• Bulb is OFF but WARM: Switch 1 controls it.
• Bulb is OFF and COLD: Switch 3 controls it.
